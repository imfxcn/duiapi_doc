Sign-up entry: [https://www.duiapi.com/sign-up](https://www.duiapi.com/sign-up)

## Open the sign-up page

If you are on the login page, click **Sign up** at the bottom to start registration.

![DUIAPI login page sign-up entry](/images/register-entry.png)

The **Sign up** link at the bottom of the login page opens the registration flow.

Enter your email address, username, and password.

Follow the page instructions and submit the form to complete registration.

## Tips

Your email address is used for verification and notifications. Use a password that combines letters, numbers, and special characters. Keep your login credentials safe to prevent account theft.

## Log in

If you already have an account, log in at [/login](https://runapi.co/login). If you forget your password, click **Forgot password?** on the login page and reset it with your registered email address.

![DUIAPI login page](/images/login-entry.png)

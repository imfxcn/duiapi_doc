After logging in to the console, open **Wallet** in the left sidebar to buy credits.

![Wallet recharge page](/images/recharge-wallet.svg)

## Recharge steps

Select a fixed amount under **Select recharge amount**, or enter the amount you want under **Custom amount**.

Confirm the **Amount payable** at the bottom of the page, then click **Alipay** and complete payment with the Alipay QR code.

![Confirm payment page](/images/recharge-confirm.svg)

The platform charges in CNY. The recharge amount is equal to the system balance added. For example, if you pay 1 CNY, your system balance increases by 1 CNY.
